Jorge Martin qualified on pole for the Grand Prix of the Netherlands at Assen with Ai Ogura second and Marco Bezzecchi third. Ogura won the 26-lap race in 40:21.905, finishing 3.512 seconds ahead of Martin in third, while Joan Mir did not finish. In the Sprint, Raul Fernandez took victory in 20:05.310, leading Ogura by 0.362 seconds and Fabio Di Giannantonio by 1.131 seconds.
