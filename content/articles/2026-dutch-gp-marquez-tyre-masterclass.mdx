Marc Marquez turned pole into a 41:00.123 win at Assen by exploiting a unique rear Michelin allocation — softer compound with a stiffer casing — that let his Ducati GP24 hold 1'31.8s laps deep into the second stint while rivals faded. Corner‑entry speed through the high‑speed Esses was 3‑4 km/h higher than the field, a direct result of the revised aero fairing’s downforce balance at lean angle.

Factory dynamics shifted: Bagnaia finished second on a standard‑spec bike, his pace dropping 0.3s/lap after lap 12 as front‑end chatter under braking at Haarbocht limited his attack. Martin took third on the Pramac GP24, confirming satellite parity; his late‑race push on used mediums produced the fastest sector‑three times, underscoring the package’s one‑lap potency.

Championship impact: Marquez cuts the deficit to 18 points with a bike that now turns Assen’s flowing layout into a Ducati circuit. The Mugello‑homologated aero update is working; the title fight has become a technical battle.
