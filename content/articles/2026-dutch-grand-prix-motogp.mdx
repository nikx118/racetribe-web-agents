Qualifying at TT Circuit Assen placed Jorge Martin (Aprilia Racing) on pole, with Ai Ogura (Trackhouse) second and Marco Bezzecchi (Aprilia) third. Ogura converted the front‑row start into a 26‑lap Grand Prix win in 40:21.905. The Saturday sprint was taken by Raul Fernandez (Trackhouse) in 20:05.310; Fabio Di Giannantonio (VR46) finished third, 1.131 s adrift. Joan Mir (Honda) recorded a DNF.
