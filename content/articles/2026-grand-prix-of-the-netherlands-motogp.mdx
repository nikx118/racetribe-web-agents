Jorge Martin secured pole at Assen, yet Ai Ogura claimed the Grand Prix of the Netherlands victory in 40:21.905, finishing 3.512 s ahead of Martin in third. In the sprint, Raul Fernandez won in 20:05.310, beating Ogura by 0.362 s, with Fabio Di Giannantonio third at 1.131 s; Joan Mir retired.
