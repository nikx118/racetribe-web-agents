Marc Marquez secured victory at the 2026 Dutch GP for the Ducati Lenovo Team, completing the race in 41:00.123. Aprilia Racing's Marco Bezzecchi finished second, earning his maiden MotoGP podium. In the Italian GP sprint, Trackhouse MotoGP Team's Raul Fernandez took the win in 19:28.408, with Jorge Martin second for Aprilia in 19:29.697.
